## Readers
Founders, CEOs, operations and product leaders at startups and SMEs, plus
digital-transformation owners at larger organisations. Smart, busy, often not engineers.

## Voice
- Practical and specific. Every post should leave the reader able to make a better decision or take a next step.
- Warm, confident, plain English. No hype, no buzzword stacks, no "In today's fast-paced world".
- Honest about trade-offs, costs and when NOT to build something.
- Short paragraphs, clear subheadings, lists where they genuinely help.

## Hard rules
- Never invent facts: no made-up statistics, studies, survey numbers, client names,
  case studies, testimonials or quotes. If a number helps, give a clearly labelled
  rough range or rule of thumb, never a fake citation.
- Comparisons that name other companies, products or prices: only state what your
  research supports, link the source, date it ("as of <month year>"), stay fair,
  and explain when each option is the better choice. Never disparage competitors.
- Never claim specific Ramlatech projects or results.
- No medical, legal or financial advice beyond general business guidance.
- Do not write a closing sales pitch or "contact us" section — the page adds its own call to action.
- English only.
